RARITY GUIDE

HOW RARE WAS YOUR ROLL?

In Sandbox, every integer from 0 through 1,000,000 has exactly the same chance: 1 in 1,000,001. Badges do not change your roll—they simply spot patterns in the number you already got. Trait Cases use their disclosed restricted pools instead.

NUMBER RARITY

Your roll’s overall rarity is based on its total EP compared with all 1,000,001 possible rolls.

  • TrashBottom 1%
  • CommonBottom 50%
  • UncommonTop 50–25%
  • RareTop 25–10%
  • EpicTop 10–5%
  • AnomalyTop 5–1%
  • MythicTop 1%

BADGE RARITY

Each badge’s tier is based on how often that individual pattern appears. Rarer badges award more EP.

  • Common>10% of rolls
  • Uncommon1–10%
  • Rare0.1–1%
  • Epic0.01–0.1%
  • Anomaly0.001–0.01%
  • Mythic<0.001% · 1 in 100,000+

TRAIT CASES

Each built-in case has a distinct theme and audited pool. Exactly 20,000 of 1,000,001 trigger values start a second spin: standard cases use a Gold Coin, while the 10 Caseception specials open a separately weighted four-trait inner case. Both paths are included in the displayed final odds and fair price. Every 1,000 EP always pays 1 CR.

CASE BATTLES

Every player opens the selected case each round and the combined virtual payout forms the winner’s pot. Gold Coins and inner cases visibly trigger their appropriate bonus lane before scoring continues. Classic uses cumulative EP, Crazy rewards the lower cumulative EP, Clutch uses each player’s best single pull, and Terminal uses only the final pull.

Badges stack. The machine begins with six reels; leading zeroes vanish after landing, and the seventh reel appears only for 1,000,000.
